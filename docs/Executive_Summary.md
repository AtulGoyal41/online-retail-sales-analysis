# Executive Summary — Online Retail II Sales Analysis

We analyzed sales data for an online retail/e-commerce gift shop, focusing on revenue reconciliation, margin leakage from cancellations, top-performing products, and customer behavior patterns.

**Net Sales Revenue** — after excluding cancellations, vouchers, and non-product entries — came out to **£9,590,901.39**.

## Key Findings

- **EIRE's revenue is concentrated in just 3 real, identifiable accounts**, creating a severe customer concentration risk.
- **The cancellation rate is significantly higher among Registered customers** than Guest customers.
- **Sales peak in November**, ahead of Christmas, rather than in December itself.

## Recommendations

1. Assign dedicated account management to the three real EIRE accounts, given their outsized concentration risk.
2. Ensure inventory and staffing are fully prepared before November, rather than in anticipation of a December rush.
